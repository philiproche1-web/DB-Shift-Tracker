## Top 10, ranked by real-world impact on a driver on shift

1. **Roster data ships twice and re-downloads 187 KB on every open** (`cache: "no-store"` defeats caching outright). Directly costs the target user data and load time, every single day. *(§6)*
2. **Migration `0005` — the security hardening — is not committed to git.** Live DB is presumably fine, but the repo no longer describes a secure database. Verify against the live DB and commit it. *(§5)*
3. **Today's duty sits exactly on the fold — measured y=632 against a 640px fold.** The driver must scroll to read their own report time, every time. *(§1)*
4. **Bottom nav fails WCAG AA in light theme — measured 4.05:1 against a 4.5:1 requirement**, at 10px, in the theme used in bright sunlight. *(§2)*
5. **Split the remaining deploy in two: client changes first, web push entirely separate.** Push is a server-side subsystem that has never run against a real driver, and its failures are invisible from the app. *(§7)*
6. **No client error monitoring and no error boundary.** A render throw is a white screen, and you find out from a driver. *(§6)*
7. **Notification permission is requested cold on first Home render**, and a reflex "Deny" silently disables reminders forever. *(§1)*
8. **`audit_log` stores two full copies of the driver's entire dataset per write, with no retention.** A full table means writes fail, which means logging a shift fails. *(§5)*
9. **OS font scaling does nothing — verified.** Root 16px→24px left body text at 12px, and 115 of 155 text nodes are ≤12px. An older driver who turned text size up gets no benefit at all. *(§2)*
10. **`rosterbuddy.ie` has no DNS records** — the domain is registered and configured in Netlify, but its nameservers (Register365) hold an empty zone, so nothing resolves and SSL can't provision. Registrar-side fix, see below. *(§7)*

*Just below the cut, and still worth doing: the client/server rest-day duplication with no parity test (§3, §7) — it matters most at the moment push actually ships.*

**Quick wins worth doing regardless:** delete the 170 MB of stale worktrees and gitignore them (§4), fix the corrupted-data screen's reference to the removed backup feature (§1), and escape the PDF export's interpolated text (§5).
