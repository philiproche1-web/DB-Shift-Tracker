## Timeline of events as recorded

- **16/10/2025, ~20:00** — Accident at the terminus, Swords Manor. Philip
  Roche pulled a muscle in his neck turning to check the bus's internal
  destination scrolls after keying his details into the Wayfarer machine.
- **16/10/2025** — Accident first reported to Central Control. Driver
  declined an ambulance. Driver waited for the Chief Inspector to attend
  the scene; the Chief Inspector (Dave Doyle) arrived roughly an hour
  after the initial call.
- **16/10/2025** — Driver states he was initially told he would be dropped
  home; this changed on arrival at the garage, and he drove himself to his
  sister's in Ashbourne instead.
- **30/10/2025** — Employee statement (G02/37) and employer accident form
  (G02/9) both signed by Philip Roche and countersigned by the manager.
- **30/10/2025** — HSA IR1 statutory notification submitted by Andrew
  Corrigan (Depot Administrator), recording anticipated incapacity of
  "one month – less than three months" and that the employee was not
  back at work as of the notification date.

## Grievance procedure reference (from company policy)

If a formal grievance is raised about how the accident/aftermath was
handled, the agreed Dublin Bus procedure (see full excerpt linked above)
sets these timelines:

1. **Informal stage** — raise with supervisor/manager first.
2. **Stage 1 (Formal)** — written complaint to immediate
   supervisor/manager. Meeting held within **14 days**. Summary note
   signed by all present. Determination issued within a further
   **7 days** (extendable by 7 days if witnesses/other parties must be
   interviewed).
3. **Stage 2 (Appeal)** — written appeal to HR Services Manager within
   **7 days** of the Stage 1 determination, clearly outlining the grounds
   of appeal. Meeting held within **10 days**. Response/determination
   issued within **7 days** of the meeting.
4. **External procedures** — if the response is inadequate, the grievance
   may be referred to the appropriate external body (e.g. Workplace
   Relations Commission).

No formal grievance has been logged in this folder yet — the note above
is a reference for if/when one is raised, based on the facts recorded in
the documents above.
